# CAN OS Operating Model

**Status:** Canonical institutional responsibility model and product direction. Legal, regulatory, contractual, and jurisdiction-specific arrangements require separate approval and cannot be inferred from this document.

## Institutional boundaries

| Actor | Intended responsibility | Not automatically authorized |
| --- | --- | --- |
| CAN / academic institution | Academic model, approved curriculum, instructional content, academic records, registrar and credentialing services where CAN is the school of record | Responsibility for every local facility or incident solely because CAN provides software |
| Church / local host | Physical space, onsite supervision, local community, site safety and operational staffing | Issuing CAN credentials, overriding academic rules, or accessing records outside granted scope |
| Site Admin | Final local operating authority for site configuration, launch work, website content, and delegated local roles | Overriding institutional authorization, safeguarding rules, privacy, or law |
| Denominational network | Approved network governance and distinctives, partner coordination, optional approved religious content | Unreviewed access to independent sites or student records |
| Academic provider / content publisher | Versioned instruction, assessments, learning resources, rights and attribution | Automatically becoming the credential issuer or local Site Admin |
| Teacher / instructor | Academic accountability, feedback and intervention within assigned scope | Physical supervision without an explicit onsite assignment |
| Room Facilitator | In-person supervised day, attendance and operational escalation | Automatically becoming a live classroom teacher or academic credential issuer |
| Student and guardian | Eligible curricular choices, learning, progress, consent and communication | Unrestricted access to other students' records |

## School of record

The school of record, academic provider, credit issuer, and local operating site must be modeled explicitly for each Program and jurisdiction. In the intended CAN-operated model, CAN is the school of record and is responsible for academic credentials and diploma issuance, while the church provides the physical learning environment. Alternative partnerships must be represented explicitly rather than assumed.

## School lifecycle

1. **Express interest and qualify.** Capture institutional and local partner context; distinguish interest from authorization.
2. **Establish a school profile.** Create a minimal canonical Site stub under authorized institutional topology. Do not automatically create a Program, publish a website, or assert opening authority.
3. **Prepare in Launch.** Configure website, team, families, academic offerings, facilities, compliance and readiness as separate capabilities.
4. **Authorize opening.** Apply institutional, legal, safeguarding, and operational gates independently of card completion.
5. **Operate in Run.** Support students, families, staff, academics, site day, records and communications.
6. **Maintain and evolve.** Site settings and ongoing readiness remain accessible without relaunching the institution.

## Network customization

Denominational academies may carry approved theological distinctives, governance, curriculum overlays, and optional prerecorded network-leadership messages. Such content does not change the asynchronous-first instructional principle or automatically grant network-wide record access. Independent churches receive a coherent standalone offering without denominational governance controls.

# CAN OS: Product Vision

**Status:** Canonical product intent. **Scope:** CAN OS and CAN Academy, independent of retired delivery initiatives.

CAN OS is the shared operating platform for a distributed Christian education network. CAN provides centralized academic and institutional infrastructure; authorized local partners provide physical community, supervision, and site operations. The exact academic provider, credit issuer, school of record, and legal responsibilities are explicit per Program and jurisdiction, never inferred from a brand or church affiliation.

## Core principles

1. **One connected experience.** School launch, recruitment, admissions, enrollment, learning, support, onsite operations, records, and communication share canonical identity and authorized domain services.
2. **Asynchronous-first instruction.** Reusable teacher-produced instructional content is the default. Live academic support, tutoring, and interventions are available without requiring scheduled live lectures.
3. **Student choice within approved offerings.** Students should be able to select eligible curriculum and instructional presenters, subject to program, licensing, placement, and academic rules. Do not confuse the onsite facilitator with the instructional content producer or the accountable teacher.
4. **Local authority with institutional boundaries.** The Site Admin leads local site configuration and operations, including the public website. Institutional authorization, safeguarding, privacy, credentialing, and legal obligations remain independently enforced.
5. **Launch then Run.** A minimal school profile establishes a site; a capability-based Launch Hub supports website creation, team invitations, recruitment, compliance, and readiness. Launch is distinct from authorization to open. After launch, normal work moves to Run; setup remains accessible through settings.
6. **Repeatable network model.** Churches, independent schools, consortia, and denominational networks may have distinct approved configuration and governance, without duplicating the platform.
7. **Configuration over forks.** Common domain models, permissions, services, and reusable workflows are preferred to partner-specific code.
8. **Evidence over appearance.** A rendered page, deployed build, or passing unit test does not prove a connected workflow or production readiness.

## Product authority versus implementation evidence

This document defines intended product direction. Current source code, migrations, and executed tests establish what exists today. When they diverge, record the gap and resolve it deliberately; neither silently overwrites the other.

## Boundaries

The one-hour launch aspiration concerns initial configuration, not automatic legal or operational authorization. Future markets and offerings are strategic possibilities, not current commitments unless separately approved.

## Documentation migration

Historical FullFlow specifications are reference evidence only. They are not active release instructions or product authority. Promote their useful requirements only after review.
